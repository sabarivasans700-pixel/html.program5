Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the way people interact with technology. It enables computers to perform tasks such as learning, problem-solving, and decision-making with greater efficiency.

AI is widely used in healthcare, education, banking, and transportation. Virtual assistants, recommendation systems, and self-driving vehicles are common examples of AI applications in everyday life.

Machine learning, a branch of AI, allows computers to improve their performance by analyzing data and recognizing patterns. This helps businesses make better decisions and provide personalized services to customers.

As AI continues to evolve, it creates new opportunities for innovation while also raising important discussions about ethics, privacy, and responsible use. Understanding AI helps individuals prepare for the future of technology.
